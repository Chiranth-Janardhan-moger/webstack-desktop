Built for Speed, Privacy, and Control

Everything a bookmark manager should be. Simple, fast, and completely under your control.

100% Private Peer-to-Peer

Your bookmarks transfer directly between your phone and PC over your home Wi-Fi. Nothing is ever sent to a remote server, and no cloud account is required.

Ultra-Lightweight (~3.6 MB)

Unlike heavy apps that bundle a 150 MB Chromium browser, WebStack uses your operating system's native web engine. It opens in milliseconds and uses under 40 MB of RAM.

Permanently Offline

Your library is stored locally on your devices. You can search, browse, and organize all your bookmarks even when traveling without an internet connection.

How to Sync in 30 Seconds

Zero accounts. No sign-in. Connect in three simple steps.

01

Turn on Sharing

Open WebStack on your Android phone, go to Settings → PC Companion, and toggle sharing ON.

02

Open WebStack on PC

Launch WebStack on your computer. It automatically discovers your phone over your local Wi-Fi.

03

Enter Your PIN

Enter the 6-digit PIN shown on your phone. Your entire bookmark library replicates instantly.
