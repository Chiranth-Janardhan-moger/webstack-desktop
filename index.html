Quickstart

How to Sync in 30 Seconds

Zero account setup. No cloud login. Connect in three simple steps.

01

Start Phone Companion

Open WebStack on your Android phone. Go to Settings → PC Companion and toggle Local Wi-Fi Sharing to ON.

02

Open WebStack Desktop

Launch the downloaded WebStack.exe on your PC. It will search your local Wi-Fi network for your phone companion.

03

Enter 6-Digit PIN

Enter the 6-digit PIN shown on your phone screen. Your entire bookmark library syncs instantly and remains available offline.

Why WebStack

Built for Speed, Privacy, and Control

Everything an offline bookmark manager should be, without the bloated browser extensions or cloud lock-in.

Ultra-Lightweight (~3.6 MB)

Unlike typical Electron desktop apps that bundle an entire 150 MB Chromium browser, WebStack uses Tauri v2 and native WebView2. Starts instantly and uses under 40 MB RAM.

100% Private Peer-to-Peer

Your bookmarks never touch a third-party server. Synchronization occurs directly between your phone and PC over your home Wi-Fi network.

Instant Keyboard Search

Press / to search hundreds of bookmarks instantly by title, URL, or domain. Filter by custom categories with number keys.

100% Offline Vault

Even when your phone is turned off or you are traveling without internet, your synced bookmarks remain permanently cached in your local desktop vault.
